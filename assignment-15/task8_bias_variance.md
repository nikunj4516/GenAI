# Task 8: Bias and Variance

## 1. What is Bias?

Bias is the error caused when a machine learning model makes strong assumptions and fails to capture patterns in the data.

High bias generally leads to underfitting.

---

## 2. What is Variance?

Variance refers to the sensitivity of a model to small changes in training data.

High variance generally leads to overfitting.

---

## 3. Relationship with Underfitting and Overfitting

Underfitting:
- High Bias
- Low Variance
- Poor training performance
- Poor testing performance

Overfitting:
- Low Bias
- High Variance
- Excellent training performance
- Poor testing performance

---

## 4. How to Reduce Overfitting?

- Use more training data
- Reduce model complexity
- Apply regularization
- Feature selection
- Cross validation
- Ensemble methods
- Early stopping
